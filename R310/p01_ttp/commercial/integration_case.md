# Integration Case — P-01

**Candidate:** P-01 — Predictive Occlusion-Isolation Controller
**Authority:** Article XXXVI §2 C13

---

## 1. Buyer archetype: Large shunt OEM (Medtronic, Integra)

### 1.1 Strategic fit
- Adds predictive multi-segment drainage to existing programmable valve portfolio
- Leverages buyer's existing manufacturing, regulatory, and clinical infrastructure
- Differentiates against competitors on software+control (not just valve mechanics)

### 1.2 Integration path
- Year 1: V0 bench prototype (buyer's engineering team)
- Year 2-3: V1 implantable development (buyer's R&D)
- Year 4-6: Pre-clinical + clinical trial
- Year 7-8: PMA filing + launch

### 1.3 Buyer-specific value
- Annual value (base case): $80M
- Based on: 15% capture of buyer's installed base, 30% revision rate reduction, $42.5K per revision cost
- Path to BUYER_VERIFIED: buyer's actual revision rate data under NDA

## 2. Buyer archetype: Mid-size shunt OEM (Sophysa, Miethke)

### 2.1 Strategic fit
- Provides differentiation vs. larger competitors (Medtronic, Integra)
- Smaller R&D budget may require partnership for V1 implantable development

### 2.2 Integration path
- Year 1: V0 bench prototype (buyer's engineering team)
- Year 2-3: V1 implantable development (partnership with contract manufacturer)
- Year 4-7: Pre-clinical + clinical trial
- Year 8-9: PMA filing + launch

### 2.3 Buyer-specific value
- Annual value (base case): $25M
- Based on: 15% capture of smaller installed base
- Path to BUYER_VERIFIED: buyer's actual market share data under NDA

## 3. Buyer archetype: CNS biotech (acquisition target)

### 3.1 Strategic fit
- P-01 adds an implantable device platform to a CNS biotech's pipeline
- May be acquired for the control law + simulator + V0 design, with V1+ development outsourced

### 3.2 Integration path
- Year 1: Due diligence + acquisition
- Year 2-3: V1 implantable development (acquired team + partnerships)
- Year 4-7: Pre-clinical + clinical trial
- Year 8-9: PMA filing + launch or partnership with larger OEM

### 3.3 Buyer-specific value
- Annual value (base case): $50M (assumes pipeline fit analysis)
- Path to BUYER_VERIFIED: buyer's pipeline fit analysis under NDA

## 4. Common integration requirements

All buyer archetypes require:
1. Engineering team capable of reproducing the simulator (1 engineer, 1-2 weeks)
2. Hardware team capable of V0 bench prototype assembly (1 engineer + 1 technician, 3-6 months)
3. Regulatory team for pathway assessment
4. IP team for counsel questions review
5. Clinical team for trial design (when applicable)

## 5. What we do NOT provide

- V1 implantable hardware development (buyer's responsibility)
- Pre-clinical animal studies (buyer's responsibility)
- Clinical trial execution (buyer's responsibility)
- Regulatory filing (buyer's responsibility)
- Guarantee of clinical success (we commit to honest disclosure, not guaranteed outcomes)
