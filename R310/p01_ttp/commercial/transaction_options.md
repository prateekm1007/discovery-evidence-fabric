# Transaction Options — P-01

**Candidate:** P-01 — Predictive Occlusion-Isolation Controller
**Authority:** Article XXXVI §2 C14; R275 STRATEGIC SHIFT (rights differentiate price, not evidence)

---

## 1. Core principle

Per R275: **The technology-transfer package is identical at every price tier.** The price changes the rights and scope, NOT the evidence quality.

The buyer receives the same complete TTP, same simulator, same raw results, same V0 bench design, same know-how transfer, regardless of price tier. What changes is the exclusivity and field-of-use.

## 2. Price tiers

### Tier 1: Strategic ($500K+ upfront + royalties)
- **Rights:** Exclusive, all fields, global
- **Field of use:** All CSF management (hydrocephalus + idiopathic intracranial hypertension + normal pressure hydrocephalus + CSF leak management)
- **Geography:** Global
- **Exclusivity:** Exclusive (no other licensees)
- **Royalties:** 3-5% of net sales
- **Milestone payments:** $1-5M at PMA approval, $5-10M at $50M cumulative sales
- **Sublicense rights:** Buyer may sublicense to affiliates
- **Buyer commitment:** Buyer commits to V1 development within 12 months, clinical trial within 36 months

### Tier 2: Strong ($100-500K upfront + royalties)
- **Rights:** Non-exclusive OR field-exclusive
- **Field of use:** Hydrocephalus shunts only
- **Geography:** Global OR regional (negotiable)
- **Exclusivity:** Field-exclusive (no other hydrocephalus licensees; we may license in other fields)
- **Royalties:** 5-8% of net sales
- **Milestone payments:** $500K-2M at PMA approval
- **Sublicense rights:** Buyer may sublicense to affiliates with our consent
- **Buyer commitment:** Buyer commits to V1 development within 18 months

### Tier 3: Narrower ($0-500K upfront)
- **Rights:** Specific use case license
- **Field of use:** Specific patient population (e.g., pediatric hydrocephalus only, or adult NPH only)
- **Geography:** Specific region (e.g., US only, EU only)
- **Exclusivity:** Non-exclusive (we may license to others in other fields/regions)
- **Royalties:** 8-12% of net sales
- **Milestone payments:** None
- **Sublicense rights:** No sublicensing without separate agreement
- **Buyer commitment:** Buyer commits to V0 bench within 6 months

## 3. What is identical at every tier

- Complete TTP folder (all 20 criteria per Article XXXVI §2)
- Reference implementation + simulator code + raw results
- V0 bench prototype design
- Tuned parameters (know-how, under NDA)
- Engineering consultation (40 hours for Tier 1, 20 hours for Tier 2, 10 hours for Tier 3)
- Differentiation dossier
- Economic model with evidence-tier labels
- Counsel questions

## 5. Why this structure (R275 rationale)

The CEO's R275 strategic shift established that we are a technology company, not a patent office. The same evidence supports every transaction; what changes is the strategic relationship:

- Tier 1 buyers want to own the space entirely — they pay for exclusivity.
- Tier 2 buyers want a strong position in their core market — they pay for field exclusivity.
- Tier 3 buyers want to test the technology in a specific niche — they pay lower upfront but higher royalties.

No buyer is "penalized" with weaker evidence. The evidence is the same. The rights reflect the buyer's strategic intent.

## 6. Negotiation parameters

Within each tier, the following are negotiable:
- Exact upfront amount (within range)
- Exact royalty rate (within range)
- Milestone structure (within range)
- Consultation hours (within reason)

The following are NOT negotiable:
- The complete TTP is the same at every tier
- The evidence-tier labels (MODELLED / PUBLICLY_VERIFIED / BUYER_VERIFIED) are honest
- The honest limitations (Article XV disclosure) are part of the package
- The path to PUBLICLY_VERIFIED (V0 bench + clinical trial) is documented

## 7. Termination rights

Either party may terminate if:
- Buyer fails to meet development commitments (Tier 1: 12 months to V1; Tier 2: 18 months; Tier 3: 6 months to V0)
- We fail to provide the agreed consultation hours
- Material breach of NDA

On termination:
- Buyer's rights revert to us
- Buyer may continue using any developed IP for internal research only (not commercial)
- Know-how remains under NDA
