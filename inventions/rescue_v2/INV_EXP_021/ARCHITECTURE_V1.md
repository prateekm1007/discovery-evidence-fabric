# Architecture V1 — INV_EXP_021

**Type**: mechanism
**Description**: The hydrogel matrix is reinforced with nanofibers that are covalently bonded to the hydrogel matrix through a click reaction, creating a stronger and more stable interface.
**Material difference**: The click reaction creates a covalent bond between the nanofibers and the hydrogel matrix, which is more stable than the non-covalent interactions in the prior art.
**New effect**: The covalent bond between the nanofibers and the hydrogel matrix enhances the mechanical strength and reduces the impact of polymer chain scission.
