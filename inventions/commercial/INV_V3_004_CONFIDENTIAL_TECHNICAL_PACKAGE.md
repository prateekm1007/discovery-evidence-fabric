## Invention Title
Thermal Mass Reduction in Active Electrode for Electrosurgical Unit


## Inventive Nucleus
Thermally enhanced thermocouples with high-thermal-conductivity materials integrated into the active electrode, and a heat-resistant, thermally compatible coating to prevent degradation and damage.


## Problem
Lateral thermal spread and tissue damage during electrosurgical procedures


## Baseline
Standard electrosurgical units with uninsulated active electrodes


## Documented Failure
THERMAL_FAILURE: Insufficient thermal mass reduction in active electrode leading to excessive lateral thermal spread and tissue damage


## System Architecture
Active electrode with integrated thermal mass reduction features, electrosurgical unit with control logic and power supply


### Components

- Active electrode with thermal mass reduction features
- Electrosurgical unit with control logic and power supply

### Materials

- Active electrode: Copper with a thermal conductivity of 386 W/m-K, Insulation: Ceramic with a thermal conductivity of 1.5 W/m-K

### Control Logic

- Logic step1: Monitor temperature at the active electrode and adjust power supply accordingly
- Logic step2: Implement thermal mass reduction features based on user input

## Causal Mechanism
The integration of thermal mass reduction features into the active electrode reduces lateral thermal spread and tissue damage by dissipating heat more efficiently

## Simulation Iterations


### v0

- Nucleus: Thermal mass reduction in the active electrode limits lateral thermal spread and tissue damage.
- Baseline: Significant lateral thermal spread and tissue damage due to high thermal mass of the active electrode
- Modified: Reduced lateral thermal spread and minimal tissue damage due to thermal mass reduction in the active electrode
- Predicted: 25% reduction in tissue damage and 30% reduction in thermal spread
- Failure: Insufficient thermal conductivity in the thermocouples, leading to inaccurate temperature readings and potential over-heating of the active electrode
- Refinement: Upgraded thermocouples with high-thermal-conductivity materials and repositioning of thermocouples within the active electrode. — This addresses the failure by increasing the thermal conductivity of the thermocouples, enabling accurate temperature readings and preventing over-heating of the active electrode.

### v1

- Nucleus: Thermally enhanced thermocouples with high-thermal-conductivity materials integrated into the active
- Baseline: The active electrode operates at a fixed temperature, leading to inconsistent thermal spread and reduced precision in temperature monitoring.
- Modified: The thermally enhanced thermocouples with high-thermal-conductivity materials embedded within the active electrode allow for real-time temperature monitoring and precise control over thermal spread.
- Predicted: Improved temperature monitoring and control by 25% with a 10% reduction in thermal spread variability.
- Failure: The high-thermal-conductivity materials may degrade over time, reducing their effectiveness, or the thermocouples may be damaged by excessive heat.

### v2

- Nucleus: Thermally enhanced thermocouples with high-thermal-conductivity materials integrated into the active
- Baseline: The thermocouple experiences significant thermal resistance and degradation due to the heat generated by the active electrode, leading to reduced accuracy and lifespan.
