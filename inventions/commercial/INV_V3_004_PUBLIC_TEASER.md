## Opportunity

A medical device modification addressing THERMAL FAILURE in Electrosurgical Unit.

## Problem Addressed

Electrosurgical units (ESUs) are medical devices used to cut and coagulate tissue during surgical procedures. They work by applying high-frequency electrical energy to the target area, which generates heat and causes tissue damage. The thermal mass of the active electrode is a known factor that contributes to the lateral thermal spread and tissue damage during electrosurgical procedures. [EVIDENCE]

## Value Proposition

Potential for significant cost savings through reduced electrode replacement and improved procedure efficiency, with a potential return on investment of 200-300% over 5 years [HYPOTHESIS]
