# Scope Law (narrowed, R510)

**Status:** RATIFIED 2026-09-19 (Round R510) — enacted into constitution v2.10.0 per the operator's 2026-09-19 governance directive (constitutional update); see R510/constitution/AMENDMENT_RECORD.json
**Sponsor:** Operator governance directive, 2026-09-19, section WHAT I WOULD REMOVE OR SIMPLIFY, verbatim rule text below.

## Operator's stated gap

The scope law forbids all engine delta while a battery is in flight. This is correct and essential during flight. But the rule has been extended to mean "no engine delta until the bottleneck is identified by harvest" — which can mean weeks of no code improvement while waiting for a battery. The scope law should apply to the measurement instrument and the scored problem set, not to the entire engine.

## The rule (replaces the prior working-law scope formulation)

```text
During a battery run: no changes to (a) the frozen
problem manifest, (b) the yield instrument, (c) the
harvest rules, (d) the clarification text for any
in-flight problem, or (e) any module that changes the
semantic output of in-flight sessions. Between battery
runs: fixes to discovery-side modules (evidence retrieval,
mechanism synthesis, candidate generation) are permitted
and encouraged, subject to all other constitutional
requirements including Art. V battery requirements for
engine changes.
```

## Provenance note (coder-recorded, Art. XV)

The prior scope law lived as round working-law (handoff records), not as a numbered constitutional article; this file constitutionalizes its narrowed replacement without inventing article numbers. The contamination protection is the frozen problem manifest and yield instrument, not the engine freeze.
