## The verdict

Keep ~85–90% of the Constitution. Do NOT remove the core epistemic protections
(evidence precedes assertion; verifier does not trust claimant; LLM untrusted; unknown
remains unknown; search count ≠ evidence; problem existence gate; no self-certification;
implementation ≠ proof; fail closed; reality boundary; buyer reality; no quota filling;
reviewer provenance; English-only operational artifacts; one canonical state; production
exact SHA; visual compiler requirement). The audit proved several of them WORK (honest
records, fail-closed visual gating, deployment identity, provenance across the container
boundary). The problem: several principles are written as LOCAL safety rules, and their
conjunction made the GLOBAL mission unreachable (Article XXVII implemented as "never
produce numbers" → Article LX engineering representability unreachable → Article LXXII
dependent on a dead capability). The Constitution answered "how do we prevent Toscanini
from lying?" and must now answer equally strongly "how do we prevent Toscanini from
becoming so constrained that it can no longer discover anything?"
