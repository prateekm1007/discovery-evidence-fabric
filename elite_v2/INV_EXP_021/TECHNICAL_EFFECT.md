# Technical Effect — INV_EXP_021

**Classification**: DOCUMENTED

## Problem
Hydrogels are prone to mechanical failure and degradation due to polymer chain scission, limiting their durability and performance in various applications.

## Distinguishing Feature
Incorporation of nanofibers into the hydrogel matrix

## Mechanism
The nanofibers act as reinforcing elements within the hydrogel matrix, distributing stress and preventing crack propagation

## Technical Effect
Enhanced mechanical strength and reduced impact of polymer chain scission, leading to more durable hydrogel coatings

## Effect Properties
- Unexpected: False
- Synergistic: True
- Non-linear: False
- Counterintuitive: False
