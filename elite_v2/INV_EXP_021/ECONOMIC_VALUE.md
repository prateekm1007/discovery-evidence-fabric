# Economic Value Analysis — INV_EXP_021

## Customer Problem
Hydrogel coatings often lack sufficient mechanical strength and durability, leading to premature failure due to polymer chain scission under stress or environmental factors.

## Economic Pain
Frequent replacement of coated components, increased maintenance costs, and potential product recalls due to coating failure.

## Current Cost
High replacement and maintenance costs for hydrogel-based products in medical devices, industrial applications, and consumer goods | INFERENCE

## Value Created
Enhanced mechanical strength and durability of hydrogel coatings, extending service life and reducing failure rates.

## Who Pays
Medical device manufacturers, industrial equipment producers, and consumer goods companies that use hydrogel coatings.

## Why They Pay
Reduced replacement costs, improved product reliability, extended service intervals, and potential for new applications requiring more durable hydrogel coatings.

## Adoption Barrier
Integration complexity into existing manufacturing processes, potential cost increase of nanofiber-reinforced hydrogel, and need for validation of enhanced performance in specific applications.

## Value Creation Types
COST_REDUCTION, FAILURE_REDUCTION, SERVICE_LIFE_EXTENSION
