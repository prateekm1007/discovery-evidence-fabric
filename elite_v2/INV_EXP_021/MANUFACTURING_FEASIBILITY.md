# Manufacturing Feasibility — INV_EXP_021

**Status**: SIMULATED_FEASIBLE

## Manufacturing Process
Solution casting or electrospinning followed by cross-linking of the hydrogel matrix with nanofiber reinforcement.

## Materials
Hydrogel polymers (e.g., PVA, PAAm, alginate) and nanofibers (e.g., cellulose nanofibers, carbon nanotubes, or synthetic polymer nanofibers).

## Tooling
Mixing equipment, electrospinning setup (if applicable), coating applicators, cross-linking chambers, curing ovens.

## Assembly
Layer-by-layer application or co-deposition of nanofibers and hydrogel matrix onto substrate.

## Quality Control
Mechanical testing (tensile, compression), microscopy (SEM, TEM) for nanofiber distribution, swelling ratio measurements, and accelerated aging tests for polymer chain scission resistance.

## Throughput
Moderate to high depending on coating method; electrospinning may be slower than solution casting.

## Yield
High for simple coating methods; may decrease with complex nanofiber integration.

## Supply Chain
Established for hydrogel polymers; specialized suppliers for nanofibers may be required.

## Regulatory Burden
Moderate to high depending on application (medical devices would require extensive biocompatibility testing).
