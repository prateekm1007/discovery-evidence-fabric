# Economic Value Analysis — INV_V3_002

## Customer Problem
Blood pressure monitors suffer from calibration drift over time, leading to inaccurate readings that can affect patient diagnosis and treatment decisions.

## Economic Pain
Inaccurate blood pressure monitoring can result in misdiagnosis, inappropriate treatment, increased healthcare costs, and potential liability issues for manufacturers.

## Current Cost
Annual replacement or recalibration of blood pressure monitors in clinical settings (EVIDENCE)

## Value Created
Extended device accuracy and lifespan through compensation for calibration drift

## Who Pays
Healthcare providers, hospitals, and eventually patients through insurance or direct payment

## Why They Pay
Reduced need for frequent device replacement, more accurate readings leading to better patient outcomes, and potential cost savings in healthcare

## Adoption Barrier
Integration into existing manufacturing processes, potential increase in device complexity, and need for clinical validation of improved accuracy

## Value Creation Types
COST_REDUCTION, FAILURE_REDUCTION, CLINICAL_OUTCOME_IMPROVEMENT
