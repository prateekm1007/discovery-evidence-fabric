# Technical Effect — INV_V3_001

**Classification**: DOCUMENTED

## Problem
Calibration drift in blood pressure monitors caused by environmental factors affecting pressure measurements

## Distinguishing Feature
Integration of a pressure equalization valve in the calibration chamber

## Mechanism
The valve allows the device to equalize internal pressure with the external environment, minimizing pressure differentials caused by environmental changes

## Technical Effect
Reduces calibration drift by mitigating the impact of environmental factors on pressure measurements

## Effect Properties
- Unexpected: False
- Synergistic: False
- Non-linear: False
- Counterintuitive: False
