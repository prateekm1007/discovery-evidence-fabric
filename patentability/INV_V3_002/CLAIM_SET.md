# Claim Set — INV_V3_002

**Independent Claim:**
A blood pressure monitor comprising: a blood pressure cuff; a reference pressure sensor; and an adjustable cavity design, wherein the adjustable cavity design is configured to position the reference pressure sensor at varying distances from the blood pressure cuff.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A blood pressure monitor according to claim 1, wherein the adjustable cavity design is formed by a mechanical linkage.
- A blood pressure monitor according to claim 1, wherein the adjustable cavity design is formed by a pneumatic system.
- A blood pressure monitor according to claim 1, wherein the adjustable cavity design is formed by a digital control system.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: blood pressure cuff
- B: reference pressure sensor
- C: adjustable cavity design
