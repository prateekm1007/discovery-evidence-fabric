# Claim Set — INV_EXP_041

**Independent Claim:**
A surgical stapler comprising: A jaw geometry that ensures consistent tissue compression for staple formation; and a firing mechanism that interacts with said jaw geometry to form staples, wherein the staple misfire rate is reduced by at least 30%.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said jaw geometry is modified to reduce staple misfire rate.
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said jaw geometry is designed to apply a consistent compressive force to tissue.
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said firing mechanism is mechanically linked to said jaw geometry.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: jaw geometry
- B: firing mechanism
- C: staple misfire rate
