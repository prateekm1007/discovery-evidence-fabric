# Claim Set — INV_EXP_014

**Independent Claim:**
An electrosurgical unit comprising: an active electrode; and a thermal mass reduction coating disposed on said active electrode, wherein said thermal mass reduction coating interacts with said active electrode to reduce thermal energy storage.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A dependent claim for an electrosurgical unit as claimed in claim 1, wherein said thermal mass reduction coating is selected from the group consisting of a ceramic material, a polymer material, and a carbon-based material.
- A dependent claim for an electrosurgical unit as claimed in claim 1, wherein said thermal mass reduction coating is disposed on said active electrode in a thickness of between 0.1 and 10 millimeters.
- A dependent claim for an electrosurgical unit as claimed in claim 1, wherein said electrosurgical unit is configured to reduce thermal spread and improve cutting performance.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: electrosurgical unit
- B: active electrode
- C: thermal mass reduction coating
