**Status:** NON_OBVIOUS_LIKELY

**Closest prior art:** US 2018/0151411 A1

**Motivation to combine:** A skilled person would have made this change to enhance mechanical resilience during the drug elution period, as the existing biodegradable polymer coating may not provide sufficient mechanical strength to withstand micro-particle stress.

**Reason to modify:** The existing biodegradable polymer coating may not provide sufficient mechanical strength to withstand micro-particle stress, and the addition of a thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer would address this issue.

**Reasoning:** The addition of a thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer to a biodegradable polymer coating is not obvious, as it requires a non-trivial combination of elements to achieve the desired functional result. A skilled person would have to consider the mechanical properties of the elastomeric topcoat layer and its interaction with the biodegradable polymer coating to achieve the desired effect.

**Unexpected effect:** The addition of the thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer would result in an unexpected effect of enhancing mechanical resilience during the drug elution period.
