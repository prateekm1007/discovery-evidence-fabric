# Claim Set — INV_EXP_012

**Independent Claim:**
A drug-eluting coating device, comprising: A) a biodegradable polymer coating; and B) a thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer integrated over said biodegradable polymer coating, wherein said elastomeric topcoat layer enhances mechanical resilience during the drug elution period by transferring mechanical stress to micro-particles.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A drug-eluting coating device according to claim 1, wherein said biodegradable polymer coating is selected from the group consisting of polylactic acid, polyglycolic acid, and polycaprolactone.
- A drug-eluting coating device according to claim 1, wherein said elastomeric topcoat layer has a thickness of between 1 μm and 10 μm.
- A method of manufacturing a drug-eluting coating device, comprising: providing a biodegradable polymer coating; and integrating a thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer over said biodegradable polymer coating.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: biodegradable polymer coating
- B: thin, flexible, and biodegradable elastomeric topcoat layer
