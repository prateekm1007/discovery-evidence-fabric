# Claim Set — INV_EXP_021

**Independent Claim:**
A hydrogel coating comprising a hydrogel matrix reinforced with nanofibers, wherein the nanofibers enhance the mechanical strength of the hydrogel matrix and reduce the impact of polymer chain scission.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A hydrogel coating according to claim 1, wherein the nanofibers are made of a material selected from the group consisting of carbon nanotubes, graphene, and polymeric nanofibers.
- A hydrogel coating according to claim 1, wherein the nanofibers are dispersed throughout the hydrogel matrix in an amount of at least 1% by weight.
- A method of making a hydrogel coating, comprising the steps of: (a) preparing a hydrogel matrix; (b) adding nanofibers to the hydrogel matrix; and (c) curing the resulting mixture to form a hydrogel coating according to claim 1.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: hydrogel coating
- B: nanofibers
