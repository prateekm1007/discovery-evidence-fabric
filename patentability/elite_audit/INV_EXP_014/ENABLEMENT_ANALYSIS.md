# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_014

**Status:** MODERATE

**Rationale:** The claim does not provide sufficient detail about the thermal mass reduction coating or how it interacts with the active electrode to reduce thermal energy storage. A person of ordinary skill in the art would need to conduct extensive experimentation to determine the appropriate coating materials, thickness, application methods, and how these parameters affect thermal energy storage. The claim also doesn't specify how the coating maintains its properties during electrosurgical procedures or how it affects the electrode's cutting and coagulation performance.

**Can survive:** False

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
