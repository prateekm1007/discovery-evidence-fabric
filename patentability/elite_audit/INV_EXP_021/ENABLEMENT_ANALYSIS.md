# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_021

**Status:** WEAK

**Rationale:** The claim does not provide sufficient detail about the specific nanofiber materials, their concentration, orientation, or method of incorporation into the hydrogel matrix. A PHOSITA would need to conduct undue experimentation to determine the optimal nanofiber type, concentration, and processing method to achieve the claimed benefits of enhanced mechanical strength and reduced polymer chain scission impact. The specification would need to provide more detailed guidance on these parameters to enable one to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.

**Can survive:** True

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
