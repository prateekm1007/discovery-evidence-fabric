# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_021

**Status:** MODERATE

**Rationale:** The claim may lack enablement because it doesn't specify critical parameters such as the type of nanofibers, their concentration, orientation, or method of incorporation into the hydrogel matrix. A person of ordinary skill in the art would need to conduct extensive experimentation to determine the optimal nanofiber type (e.g., cellulose, chitosan, synthetic polymers), concentration (e.g., 0.1-10% w/w), dispersion method, and crosslinking conditions to achieve the desired mechanical enhancement and scission reduction. The claim also doesn't specify the hydrogel composition or the testing methods used to demonstrate the claimed benefits, making it difficult to reproduce the invention without undue experimentation.

**Can survive:** True

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
