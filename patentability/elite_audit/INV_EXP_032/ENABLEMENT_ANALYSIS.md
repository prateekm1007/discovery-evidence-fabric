# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_032

**Status:** NONE

**Rationale:** The claim describes a pulse oximeter with an optical filter, LED, and photodiode arranged to reduce wavelength drift. While the claim doesn't provide detailed specifications, these are standard components in pulse oximetry that a person of ordinary skill in the art would understand how to implement. The functional relationship between the components is clearly stated, and the purpose (reducing wavelength drift to improve accuracy) is well-defined. No evidence suggests undue experimentation would be required.

**Can survive:** True

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
