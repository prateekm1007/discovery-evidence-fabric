# Design-Around Analysis — INV_EXP_032

**Attack strength:** MODERATE

**Easiest competitor workaround:** An easy design around would be to use a wavelength-stabilized LED that inherently minimizes drift without requiring a separate optical filter. Modern LEDs can be manufactured with built-in wavelength stability through improved semiconductor materials and manufacturing processes. This approach would eliminate the need for the optical filter component while still achieving the goal of reducing wavelength drift. The value proposition of improved measurement accuracy could still be maintained, though potentially at a higher component cost. This design around avoids the specific combination of filter-LED-photodiode arrangement claimed.

**Does competitor still obtain value?** True

**Claim refinement needed:** The claim could survive by arguing that the wavelength-stabilized LED approach doesn't achieve the same level of drift reduction as the claimed optical filter system, or that the filter approach provides additional benefits not present in the stabilized LED approach.
