# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_003

**Status:** MODERATE

**Rationale:** The claim does not provide sufficient detail about the specific formulation, processing methods, or fiber loading percentages that would enable a person of ordinary skill in the art to make and use the invention without undue experimentation. The claim only states that short carbon fibers are dispersed within the polymer matrix material to enhance mechanical properties, but doesn't specify critical parameters like fiber length, diameter, concentration, dispersion method, or polymer matrix composition. A PHOSITA would need to conduct extensive experimentation to determine the optimal formulation and processing conditions.

**Can survive:** False

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
