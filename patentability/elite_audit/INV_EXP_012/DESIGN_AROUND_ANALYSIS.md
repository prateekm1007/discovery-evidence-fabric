# Design-Around Analysis — INV_EXP_012

**Attack strength:** MODERATE

**Easiest competitor workaround:** A competitor could design around the claim by creating a drug-eluting coating with a single-layer structure that incorporates both the drug-eluting properties and mechanical resilience through a specially formulated gradient material. Alternatively, they could use a non-biodegradable but biocompatible elastomeric topcoat that provides similar mechanical benefits without the biodegradability constraint. This approach would still achieve the value of maintaining coating integrity during drug elution, though it might require different regulatory pathways.

**Does competitor still obtain value?** True

**Claim refinement needed:** The inventor could strengthen the claim by emphasizing the unique advantages of the biodegradable elastomeric topcoat over non-biodegradable alternatives, such as eliminating the need for a second surgical procedure to remove the coating after drug elution is complete.

**Rule:** For every strong claim, generate the easiest competitor workaround. If the competitor still obtains the value, DESIGN_AROUND_RISK = HIGH. Refine claim if possible.
