# Design-Around Analysis — INV_EXP_008

**Attack strength:** MODERATE

**Easiest competitor workaround:** A competitor could design around the claim by using alternative antimicrobial agents that are not chlorhexidine gluconate but provide similar or better antimicrobial protection. Examples include silver nanoparticles, iodine-based compounds, or other slow-release antimicrobial agents. This would avoid infringement while still achieving the value of infection prevention and extended wear time. The economic value would be maintained as the core benefit is infection prevention, not specifically chlorhexidine gluconate delivery.

**Does competitor still obtain value?** True

**Claim refinement needed:** The claim could be strengthened by adding specific features of the chlorhexidine gluconate reservoir (e.g., specific release kinetics, concentration range, or integration method) that provide unique advantages over alternative antimicrobials.

**Rule:** For every strong claim, generate the easiest competitor workaround. If the competitor still obtains the value, DESIGN_AROUND_RISK = HIGH. Refine claim if possible.
