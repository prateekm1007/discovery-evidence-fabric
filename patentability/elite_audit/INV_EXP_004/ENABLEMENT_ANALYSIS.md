# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_004

**Status:** NONE

**Rationale:** The claim describes a motor mount with a tuned vibration damper that reduces motor vibration and stress on bearings, increasing bearing lifespan by at least 30% and reducing motor vibration by at least 25%. The claim provides sufficient detail about the function and purpose of the invention. A person of ordinary skill in the art would understand how to implement a tuned vibration damper in a motor mount for a CPAP device based on the general knowledge of vibration damping and motor mounts, even without specific experimental details.

**Can survive:** True

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
