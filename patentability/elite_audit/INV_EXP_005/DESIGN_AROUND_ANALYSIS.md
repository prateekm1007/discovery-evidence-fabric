# Design-Around Analysis — INV_EXP_005

**Attack strength:** MODERATE

**Easiest competitor workaround:** The easiest design around would be to create a cardiac pacemaker with a semi-permeable membrane housing instead of a fully sealed, waterproof container. This would allow for some moisture exchange while still protecting internal components. The sensor could be modified to detect mechanical heart movements rather than electrical signals, though this might reduce signal detection accuracy. The control unit could remain similar but would need recalibration for the different input signal. This design around would still achieve cost reduction and durability enhancement (though potentially less than the original), but might compromise on signal detection accuracy.

**Does competitor still obtain value?** True

**Claim refinement needed:** The claim could survive by emphasizing the superior signal detection accuracy of electrical signal detection over mechanical detection, and the superior protection of a fully sealed housing over a semi-permeable membrane.
