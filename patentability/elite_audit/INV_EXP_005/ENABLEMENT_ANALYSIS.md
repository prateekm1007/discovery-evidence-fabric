**Status:** NONE

**Rationale:** The claim describes a cardiac pacemaker with specific features (sealed waterproof housing, sensor for detecting electrical heart signals, control unit for generating pacing signals). While the claim doesn't provide detailed implementation specifics, these are well-established components in the field of cardiac pacemakers. A person having ordinary skill in the art (PHOSITA) would be able to make and use such a device based on general knowledge in the field of cardiac pacemakers without undue experimentation.
