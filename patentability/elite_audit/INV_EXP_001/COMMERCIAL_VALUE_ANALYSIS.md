## Customer Problem

## Economic Pain
Inaccurate readings lead to false positives/negatives, increased testing costs, and potential safety issues in medical or environmental monitoring applications.

## Value Created
The mechanical shutter system provides an automated, stable calibration point that maintains measurement accuracy over extended periods without manual intervention.

## Who Pays
Medical device manufacturers, environmental monitoring companies, and point-of-care testing providers would pay for this technology.

## Why They Pay
Reduced calibration frequency, improved measurement accuracy, lower operational costs, and enhanced reliability of their products.
