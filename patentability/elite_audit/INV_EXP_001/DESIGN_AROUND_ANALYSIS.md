# Design-Around Analysis — INV_EXP_001

**Attack strength:** STRONG

**Easiest competitor workaround:** A competitor could easily design around this claim by using an electronic shutter (such as a liquid crystal shutter or MEMS-based optical switch) instead of a mechanical shutter. This would achieve the same value of periodic blocking of the primary LED's light for calibration while avoiding the mechanical components. Electronic shutters can be more reliable, have longer lifespans, and allow for faster switching speeds than mechanical shutters. The calibration function would still be maintained, preserving the economic value of automated, stable calibration.

**Does competitor still obtain value?** True

**Claim refinement needed:** The claim could be amended to specifically cover both mechanical and electronic shutters, or to focus on the calibration method rather than the specific shutter mechanism.
