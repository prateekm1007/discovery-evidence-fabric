# Enablement Analysis (35 USC 112) — INV_EXP_038

**Status:** MODERATE

**Rationale:** The claim does not provide sufficient detail about how the MSNP coating is loaded with Onosma armenum extract, how it's integrated onto the spinal fusion device surface, or the specific concentrations or methods used. A person of ordinary skill in the art would likely need to conduct extensive experimentation to determine the optimal loading capacity, release kinetics, and integration method to achieve the claimed benefits of enhanced implant integration and reduced biofilm formation.

**Can survive:** True

**Rule:** A PHOSITA must be able to make and use the invention without undue experimentation.
