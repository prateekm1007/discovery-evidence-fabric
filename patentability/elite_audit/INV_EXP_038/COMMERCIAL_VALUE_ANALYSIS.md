# Commercial Value Analysis — INV_EXP_038

## Customer Problem
Poor integration of spinal fusion devices with surrounding bone tissue, leading to failed fusions, revision surgeries, and increased risk of infection.

## Economic Pain
Failed spinal fusions result in prolonged patient disability, additional surgical interventions, increased healthcare costs, and potential long-term complications.

## Current Cost
Spinal fusion revision surgeries cost approximately $80,000-$150,000 per case (EVIDENCE|INFERENCE)

## Current Failure
Up to 15% of spinal fusion procedures may require revision due to non-union or complications (EVIDENCE|INFERENCE)

## Value Created
Reduced revision rates, improved fusion success, decreased infection rates, and potentially faster recovery times

## Who Pays
Healthcare systems, insurance companies, and patients through insurance premiums

## Why They Pay
Significant cost savings from avoiding revision surgeries, improved patient outcomes, reduced hospital stays, and lower complication management costs

## Adoption Barrier
Regulatory approval pathway for combination device-bioactive product, potential cost increase over conventional devices, surgeon training and familiarity with new technology

## Value Creation Types
COST_REDUCTION, OUTCOME_IMPROVEMENT, COMPLICATION_REDUCTION

## Market Size Evidence
**Tag:** INFERENCE
**Basis:** Global spinal fusion market was approximately $7.5B in 2022 with projected CAGR of 4.5%. Assuming 15% adoption rate of advanced bioactive devices could represent a $500M+ opportunity
