# Commercial Value Analysis — INV_EXP_026

## Customer Problem
Patients with knee injuries or degenerative conditions require effective surgical solutions that restore mobility and reduce pain, but current implants may not provide optimal fit or durability.

## Economic Pain
The high cost of revision surgeries when primary implants fail, combined with patient disability and reduced quality of life, creates significant economic burden on healthcare systems and individuals.

## Current Cost
Average knee replacement surgery costs $30,000-$50,000 in the US (EVIDENCE), with revision surgeries costing 50-100% more (INFERENCE).

## Current Failure
Approximately 10-20% of knee implants require revision within 10-15 years (EVIDENCE), often due to wear, loosening, or poor alignment.

## Value Created
Improved implant longevity, better patient outcomes, reduced revision rates, and potentially lower overall healthcare costs.

## Who Pays
Health insurance providers, Medicare/Medicaid, and patients (through insurance premiums or out-of-pocket costs).

## Why They Pay
Reduction in revision surgeries and associated costs, improved patient outcomes leading to lower long-term care expenses, and potential for shorter recovery times.

## Adoption Barrier
High regulatory hurdles for medical devices, surgeon training requirements, competition from established implant manufacturers, and initial cost premium.

## Value Creation Types
COST_REDUCTION, QUALITY_IMPROVEMENT, DURABILITY_ENHANCEMENT
