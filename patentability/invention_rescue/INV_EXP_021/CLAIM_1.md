## Claim Text

```
A Claim 1: 
A method of creating a hydrogel coating with improved mechanical strength and resistance to polymer chain scission, comprising:
- applying a hydrogel matrix to a substrate;
- introducing nanofibers into the hydrogel matrix through a click reaction, wherein the click reaction creates a covalent bond between the nanofibers and the hydrogel matrix;
- wherein the covalent bond between the nanofibers and the hydrogel matrix enhances the mechanical strength of the hydrogel coating and reduces the impact of polymer chain scission.
```
