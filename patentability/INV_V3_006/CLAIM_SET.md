# Claim Set — INV_V3_006

**Independent Claim:**
A method for reducing the risk of lead fracture in an implantable defibrillator, comprising: A) a polymer-jacketed, single-filar, high-strength alloy conductor; and B) a design that minimizes mechanical interaction between the lead and the surrounding tissue.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A method for reducing the risk of lead fracture in an implantable defibrillator, comprising the method of claim 1, wherein the polymer-jacketed, single-filar, high-strength alloy conductor is made of a material selected from the group consisting of titanium, stainless steel, and nitinol.
- A method for reducing the risk of lead fracture in an implantable defibrillator, comprising the method of claim 1, wherein the design that minimizes mechanical interaction between the lead and the surrounding tissue is achieved by using a curved or angled lead configuration.
- A method for reducing the risk of lead fracture in an implantable defibrillator, comprising the method of claim 1, wherein the polymer-jacketed, single-filar, high-strength alloy conductor has a tensile strength of at least 1000 MPa.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: polymer-jacketed, single-filar, high-strength alloy conductor
- B: design that minimizes mechanical interaction between the lead and the surrounding tissue
