# Obviousness Analysis (35 USC 103) — INV_V3_006

**Status:** NON_OBVIOUS_LIKELY

**Closest prior art:** US 2018/0054561 A1

**Motivation to combine:** A skilled person would have made this change to address the issue of lead fracture in implantable defibrillators, as it is a critical concern in the medical device industry. The prior art (US 2018/0054561 A1) teaches the use of high-strength alloy conductors, but does not address the mechanical interaction between the lead and surrounding tissue.

**Reason to modify:** The skilled person would have modified the design to minimize mechanical interaction between the lead and surrounding tissue to improve the longevity and reliability of the implantable defibrillator.

**Reasoning:** The combination of a polymer-jacketed, single-filar, high-strength alloy conductor and a design that minimizes mechanical interaction between the lead and surrounding tissue would not have been obvious to a skilled person, as it requires a non-trivial combination of elements and a deep understanding of the underlying technology. The skilled person would have needed to make a non-obvious connection between the two elements to arrive at the claimed invention.
