# Claim Set — INV_EXP_032

**Independent Claim:**
A pulse oximeter comprising: A) an optical filter; B) an LED; C) a photodiode; wherein said optical filter interacts with said LED and said photodiode to reduce wavelength drift, thereby improving measurement accuracy.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A pulse oximeter according to claim 1, wherein said optical filter is positioned between said LED and said photodiode.
- A pulse oximeter according to claim 1, wherein said optical filter is designed to filter out wavelengths outside a predetermined range.
- A pulse oximeter according to claim 1, wherein said photodiode is a photodetector having a sensitivity to light in the range of 600-1000 nanometers.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: optical filter
- B: LED
- C: photodiode
