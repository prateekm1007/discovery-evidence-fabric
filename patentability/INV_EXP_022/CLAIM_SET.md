# Claim Set — INV_EXP_022

**Independent Claim:**
A thermal management system for an implantable defibrillator, comprising: A) an interactive component that interacts with the electrical components of the implantable defibrillator to dissipate heat generated during device operation; and B) a heat dissipation component that is in thermal communication with the interactive component to facilitate heat transfer and slow down voltage degradation.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A thermal management system for an implantable defibrillator, said system being configured to extend the lifespan of the implantable defibrillator by reducing battery degradation.
- A thermal management system for an implantable defibrillator, said system being operable to interact with the electrical components of the implantable defibrillator to dissipate heat generated during device operation.
- A thermal management system for an implantable defibrillator, said system being capable of slowing down voltage degradation in the implantable defibrillator by dissipating heat generated during device operation.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: interactive component
- B: heat dissipation component
