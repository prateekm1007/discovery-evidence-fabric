# Claim Set — INV_V3_001

**Independent Claim:**
A blood pressure monitor comprising: A calibration chamber; B a pressure equalization valve in fluid communication with said calibration chamber, wherein said pressure equalization valve allows said calibration chamber to equalize pressure with the environment, thereby reducing the impact of environmental factors on said blood pressure monitor.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A calibration chamber for a blood pressure monitor, wherein said calibration chamber is in fluid communication with a pressure equalization valve.
- A pressure equalization valve for a blood pressure monitor, wherein said pressure equalization valve is in fluid communication with a calibration chamber and allows said calibration chamber to equalize pressure with the environment.
- A blood pressure monitor, wherein said blood pressure monitor comprises a calibration chamber and a pressure equalization valve, and wherein said calibration chamber and said pressure equalization valve are in fluid communication with each other.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: Calibration chamber
- B: Pressure equalization valve
