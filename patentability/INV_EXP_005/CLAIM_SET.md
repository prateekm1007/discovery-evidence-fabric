# Claim Set — INV_EXP_005

**Independent Claim:**
A cardiac pacemaker device for regulating heartbeats, comprising: A) a housing (A) having a structural requirement of being a sealed, waterproof container (structural_requirement); B) a sensor (B) for detecting heart activity, wherein the sensor (B) has a technical feature of being capable of detecting electrical signals (technical_feature) and a functional requirement of transmitting data to a control unit (functional_requirement); and C) a control unit (not shown) for generating pacing signals

**Dependent Claims:**
- A cardiac pacemaker device for regulating heartbeats, comprising the housing (A) of claim 1, wherein the housing (A) has a size (parameter) of 50 mm in length (parameter) and 20 mm in width (parameter);
- A cardiac pacemaker device for regulating heartbeats, comprising the sensor (B) of claim 1, wherein the sensor (B) has a sensitivity (parameter) of 0.1 mV;
- A cardiac pacemaker device for regulating heartbeats, comprising the control unit (not shown) of claim 1, wherein the control unit (not shown) has a memory (A) for storing pacing parameters (technical_feature)

**Claim Elements:**
- A: sealed, waterproof container
- B: capable of detecting electrical signals
- C: generating pacing signals
