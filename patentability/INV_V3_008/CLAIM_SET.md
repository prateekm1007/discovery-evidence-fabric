# Claim Set — INV_V3_008

**Independent Claim:**
A surgical stapler comprising: A) a staple channel with a modified geometry, wherein said modified geometry is configured to guide tissue into a precise compression zone; and B) a staple deployment mechanism, wherein said staple deployment mechanism is configured to deploy staples into said tissue after said tissue has been compressed to a consistent thickness of ≤2 mm.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said modified geometry is a curved or angled channel.
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said staple deployment mechanism is a pneumatic or hydraulic system.
- A surgical stapler according to claim 1, wherein said precise compression zone is defined by a pair of opposing surfaces that converge to form a V-shape.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: modified staple channel geometry
- B: staple deployment mechanism
- C: precise compression zone
