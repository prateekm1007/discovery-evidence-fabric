# Obviousness Analysis (35 USC 103) — INV_EXP_010

**Status:** OBVIOUSNESS_RISK

**Closest prior art:** US20110054503A1

**Motivation to combine:** A skilled person would have made this change to improve the reliability and longevity of the deep brain stimulator system by reducing stress concentration at the lead fixation point.

**Reason to modify:** The skilled person would have modified the prior art to incorporate the tapered shape, formed from a biocompatible material, and adapted to be used in conjunction with a stimulation lead, to achieve the functional requirement of reducing stress concentration.

**Reasoning:** While the prior art teaches the importance of reducing stress concentration in deep brain stimulator systems and the use of tapered shapes in medical devices, the specific parameters and the use of a biocompatible material in the claimed invention would have been considered a reasonable extension of the prior art. However, the skilled person would have had a reasonable expectation of success in modifying the prior art to achieve the claimed invention, which suggests a risk of obviousness.

**Unexpected effect:** The tapered shape of the lead fixation point would have been expected to reduce stress concentration, but the specific parameters (less than 1 mm and less than 1.5 mm) and the use of a biocompatible material would have been considered a reasonable extension of the prior art.
