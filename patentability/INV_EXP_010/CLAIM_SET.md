# Claim Set — INV_EXP_010

**Independent Claim:**
A deep brain stimulator system comprising a lead fixation point having a tapered shape, wherein the tapered shape reduces stress concentration at the lead fixation point.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A deep brain stimulator system comprising a lead fixation point having a tapered shape, wherein the tapered shape reduces stress concentration at the lead fixation point, wherein the tapered shape is defined by a radius of curvature of less than 1 mm.
- A deep brain stimulator system comprising a lead fixation point having a tapered shape, wherein the tapered shape reduces stress concentration at the lead fixation point, wherein the tapered shape is formed from a biocompatible material.
- A deep brain stimulator system comprising a lead fixation point having a tapered shape, wherein the tapered shape reduces stress concentration at the lead fixation point, wherein the tapered shape is adapted to be used in conjunction with a stimulation lead having a diameter of less than 1.5 mm.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: tapered shape
- B: tapered shape
- C: tapered shape
