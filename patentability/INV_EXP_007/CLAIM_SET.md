# Claim Set — INV_EXP_007

**Independent Claim:**
A closure device comprising: a self-expanding nitinol mesh; and a bioresorbable polymer locking mechanism integrated into said mesh, wherein said locking mechanism interacts with a septal surface to prevent displacement of said mesh.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A closure device according to claim 1, wherein said locking mechanism is configured to prevent embolization of said septal occluder device.
- A closure device according to claim 1, wherein said bioresorbable polymer locking mechanism is made from a material selected from the group consisting of polylactic acid, polyglycolic acid, and polycaprolactone.
- A closure device according to claim 1, wherein said self-expanding nitinol mesh is configured to expand to a diameter of at least 10 mm.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: self-expanding nitinol mesh
- B: bioresorbable polymer locking mechanism
