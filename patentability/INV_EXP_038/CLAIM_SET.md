# Claim Set — INV_EXP_038

**Independent Claim:**
A spinal fusion device comprising a bioactive mesoporous silica nanoparticle (MSNP) coating loaded with Onosma armenum extract integrated onto its surface, wherein the bioactive MSNP coating interacts with surrounding tissue to enhance implant integration and reduce biofilm formation.

**Dependent Claims:**
- A spinal fusion device according to claim 1, wherein the bioactive MSNP coating is formed from a mixture of Onosma armenum extract and a mesoporous silica precursor.
- A spinal fusion device according to claim 1, wherein the bioactive MSNP coating is loaded with a therapeutically effective amount of Onosma armenum extract.
- A spinal fusion device according to claim 1, wherein the bioactive MSNP coating is capable of releasing Onosma armenum extract over a period of at least 30 days.

**Claim Elements:**
- A: bioactive mesoporous silica nanoparticle (MSNP) coating
- B: Onosma armenum extract
