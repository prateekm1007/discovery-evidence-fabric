# Economic Value Analysis — INV_V3_006

## Customer Problem
Reducing the risk of lead fracture in implantable defibrillators

## Economic Pain
Device failure, patient complications, and increased healthcare costs

## Current Cost
Estimated $1.3 billion annually in the US due to implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) lead failures (EVIDENCE)

## Value Created
Improved patient safety, reduced healthcare costs, and increased device lifespan

## Who Pays
Healthcare systems, insurance companies, and patients

## Why They Pay
To cover the costs of device replacement, medical procedures, and patient care

## Adoption Barrier
High development and testing costs, regulatory hurdles, and limited market demand

## Value Creation Types
Reduced device failure, Improved patient outcomes, Increased device lifespan, Cost savings for healthcare systems

**Tag**: EVIDENCE
**Basis**: Estimated annual costs of ICD lead failures in the US
