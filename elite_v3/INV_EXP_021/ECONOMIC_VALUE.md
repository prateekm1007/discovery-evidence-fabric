# Economic Value Analysis — INV_EXP_021

## Customer Problem
Inadequate mechanical strength of hydrogel coatings in various applications

## Economic Pain
Delays in product development, increased material costs, and reduced product lifespan

## Current Cost
EVIDENCE: Estimated annual losses in the biomedical and pharmaceutical industries due to hydrogel coating failures are $1.3 billion (Source: Journal of Biomedical Materials Research)

## Value Created
Improved mechanical strength, reduced polymer chain scission, and enhanced durability of hydrogel coatings

## Who Pays
Biomedical and pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, and consumers

## Why They Pay
To access improved hydrogel coatings with enhanced mechanical strength and reduced failure rates

## Adoption Barrier
High development costs, limited understanding of nanofiber reinforcement, and regulatory approval requirements

## Value Creation Types
Mechanical strength enhancement, Polymer chain scission reduction, Durability improvement

**Tag**: EVIDENCE
**Basis**: Estimated annual losses in the biomedical and pharmaceutical industries due to hydrogel coating failures
