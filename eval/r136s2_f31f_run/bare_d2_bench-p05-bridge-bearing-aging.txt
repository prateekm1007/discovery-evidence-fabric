**Mitigation Proposal: Hybrid High-Modulus Elastomer/Spring Sandwich Bearing with Environmental Barrier**

### 1. Mechanism
The proposed design replaces the traditional 100% laminated elastomer (rubber-steel) pad with a **hybrid structural bearing**. This device combines:
   - A **core** of interlocking, low-hysteresis metal spring assemblies or high-density polyurethane (to handle vertical load and vertical stiffness).
   - **Top and bottom faces** of a thin, high-modulus, ozone-resistant thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) layer bonded to a rigid steel cover plate.
   - A **permanent environmental seal** (e.g., stainless steel sleeve or sealed polymer pot) surrounding the entire assembly to isolate the internal materials from atmospheric ozone, UV radiation, and moisture.

*Why this works:* 
   - **Vertical Stiffness:** The metal springs or hard TPE core provide high, stable vertical stiffness that does not creep like natural rubber. This directly addresses the "stiffness drift" component of the failure.
   - **Ozone Resistance:** TPE (e.g., thermoplastic elastomer based on polycarbonate or polyethylene) is inherently more resistant to ozone cracking than natural rubber (SBR/NR). The external seal acts as a redundant barrier.
   - **Creep Mitigation:** By removing the primary load-bearing elastomer, the viscoelastic creep mechanism is eliminated from the critical load path. The thin TPE layers only handle shear accommodation and minor rotation, not vertical load.

### 2. Implementation
   - **Geometry:** The bearing is cast as a monolithic hybrid unit. The core springs are encapsulated within a stainless steel or PTFE-lined steel housing. The TPE layers are injection-molded onto the housing to form the bearing surface.
   - **Sealing:** A continuous, factory-installed seal (similar to a hermetic pot) encloses the unit. This seal is designed to last 75+ years without maintenance.
   - **Installation:** The hybrid bearing is installed in place of the standard laminated pad. No additional space is required beyond the standard bearing envelope.
   - **Monitoring:** Embedded fiber-optic strain gauges are installed in the steel housing to monitor any micro-strain accumulation over time. This provides data for verification without requiring physical access for inspection.

### 3. Measures It Relies On
   - **Vertical Deflection Drift:** The stiffness of the metal springs and TPE core is governed by elastic modulus, which is time-invariant. Creep in metals at ambient temperatures is negligible over 75 years. TPE creep is significantly lower than natural rubber and can be calculated using linear viscoelastic models.
   - **Ozone Resistance:** TPE is selected based on ASTM G72/G97 ozone resistance standards, which are superior to natural rubber. The seal provides a physical barrier that reduces ozone concentration to near-zero levels at the material interface.
   - **No Replacement Access:** The design is sealed and passive. No moving parts require maintenance. The embedded sensors allow for remote health monitoring.

### 4. Concrete Test to Falsify the Approach
**Test:** **Long-Duration Accelerated Weathering and Creep Test**
   - **Protocol:** Subject 5 prototype hybrid bearings to an accelerated weathering chamber for 20,000 hours (equivalent to ~75 years under high ozone/UV exposure) at 60°C and 50% RH, while simultaneously applying a static vertical load equal to 1.5 times the design load.
   - **Failure Criteria (Falsification):**
     1. If the **vertical deflection drift** exceeds **10%** of the initial design deflection at the end of the 20,000-hour test, the approach fails.
     2. If **cracking** is observed in the TPE layers or the seal integrity is compromised (detected by mass loss or gas permeation testing), the approach fails.
     3. If the **stiffness drift** (change in load-deflection slope) exceeds **10%** from initial values, the approach fails.

This test directly falsifies the proposal if the hybrid system exhibits the same time-dependent deformation and environmental degradation as the original laminated elastomer bearing.
