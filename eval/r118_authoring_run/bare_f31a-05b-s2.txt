**Mitigation Proposal: Precision Metered Manual Greasing with Visual Feedback**

**Mechanism:**
Replace the standard “grease until purge” method with a **controlled, low-volume, high-frequency injection protocol** that utilizes the existing manual grease nipple to deliver precise, small quantities of the fixed OEM grease. This prevents the high internal pressure and heat generation caused by over-greasing (washover), which is the root cause of film thinning and race spallation. The mechanism relies on maintaining a stable hydrodynamic film thickness by avoiding displacement of the base oil, thereby preserving the viscosity and load-carrying capacity of the fixed grease spec.

**Implementation:**
1.  **Modify the Existing Route:** Convert the current routine from “periodic heavy greasing” to “frequent light dosing.” Using the existing manual grease gun, equip it with a **calibrated, small-diameter metering pump or a precision injector nozzle** (if not already present) to ensure consistent, small bursts of grease.
2.  **Establish a Volume Limit:** Determine the maximum safe volume per injection (e.g., 2–5 grams) based on the bearing’s internal volume, not the purge point. The operator must stop injection immediately upon reaching this calculated limit, regardless of whether grease is seen at the seals.
3.  **Increase Frequency:** Double or triple the greasing frequency to maintain film integrity without accumulating excess. For example, if previously greased weekly, move to bi-weekly or weekly small doses.
4.  **Operator Protocol:** Train the operator on the “less is more” principle. The operator is instructed to never inject until grease emerges from the seals (the traditional purge method) and to strictly adhere to the new volume/frequency schedule. Use a visual or mechanical counter on the gun to ensure consistency.

**Measures Relied On:**
*   **Pressure Reduction:** By limiting volume, the internal bearing pressure remains below the threshold that causes excessive shear heating, which degrades the grease film and accelerates spalling.
*   **Thermal Stability:** Smaller doses dissipate heat more effectively, preventing the local temperature spikes that thin the oil film and cook the grease.
*   **Film Integrity:** Maintaining the original grease spec while preventing over-greasing ensures the base oil is not displaced or emulsified, preserving the intended elastomeric structure of the grease pack.
*   **No Hardware/Spec Change:** This solution strictly adheres to the constraint by using the existing manual route and OEM grease. It only changes the *procedure* (volume/frequency) and potentially adds a low-cost metering accessory to the existing gun, not a new automated system or a new grease type.
