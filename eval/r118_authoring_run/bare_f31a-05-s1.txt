**Mitigation Proposal: Flow-Controlled Manual Greasing Protocol with Visual Drop-Point Verification**

**Mechanism:**
The failure is caused by "washover," which results from the accumulation of excess grease in the bearing space. When too much grease is present, the rotational speed of the bearing causes the grease to heat up and fluidize (shear thinning), losing its ability to support load and protect surfaces, leading to spalling. The mitigation mechanism is the strict enforcement of **volume-limited dosing** via a manual gun equipped with a positive displacement measurement system, combined with a **drop-point release verification**. This ensures that only the precise amount of grease needed to replenish minor leakage and maintain film thickness is added, preventing the hydraulic pressure buildup that forces old grease out (washover) and prevents the thermal breakdown of the fixed OEM grease pack.

**Implementation:**
1.  **Equip the Manual Gun:** Fit the existing manual greasing gun with a graduated, positive-displacement piston or a calibrated nozzle that measures the exact volume of grease expelled per stroke (e.g., a syringe-style attachment or a gun with a known-stroke-volume cylinder).
2.  **Standardize Dosing Volume:** Determine the maximum allowable volume per service interval based on the bearing manufacturer’s guidelines for the specific outer race geometry (typically 2–4 drops or 0.1–0.2 mL for small bearings, scaled up slightly for larger deck bearings). Establish a fixed "per-stroke" limit.
3.  **Visual Drop-Point Verification (The Critical Step):** Instead of pumping until "full" or "resistance drops," the operator must pump until grease is visible *just* at the seal lip or a designated bleed point (if accessible), or until a single, controlled drop appears at the drain point. If the bearing has no bleed point, the operator stops immediately after the first visible sign of displacement. **No further pumping is allowed** once the visual cue is met.
4.  **Service Interval Calibration:** Increase the frequency of this manual greasing procedure (e.g., from monthly to weekly) but drastically reduce the volume per session. This ensures the grease film is maintained without ever accumulating excess material that leads to shear heating and washover.

**Measures Relied On:**
*   **Positive Displacement Volume Measurement:** The physical limit on the gun’s stroke ensures that no more than a quantified, minimal volume can be injected, eliminating human error in "pumping until it feels full."
*   **Visual Confirmation of Displacement:** Relying on the first visible drop or seal-lip appearance ensures the bearing is filled to capacity but not over-filled, directly countering the washover mechanism.
*   **Increased Frequency, Reduced Volume:** This strategy maintains a fresh, thin grease film (preventing starvation and spalling) while preventing the accumulation of excess grease (preventing washover and thermal degradation).
*   **Compliance with Fixed Grease Spec:** Since the OEM grease cannot be changed, this method relies on the grease’s inherent high-temperature stability and shear strength, which are only preserved if the volume is controlled to prevent internal heating from over-lubrication.
