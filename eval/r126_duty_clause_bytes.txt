 DUTY-CYCLE RULE: if your corrected proposal modulates a duty cycle, an on/off or start/stop loop, or any periodic schedule (including one you introduce while restructuring the proposal), include an explicit OPERATIONAL COST line that quantifies or bounds the operational impact of that modulation - throughput, energy, or availability, with a magnitude or bound (for example 'OPERATIONAL COST: the on/off loop reduces average throughput by no more than 4%'). A modulation without a quantified or bounded operational cost is a defect. If the proposal modulates no duty cycle or schedule, add no cost line.
