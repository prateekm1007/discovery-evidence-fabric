My hypothesis is that the primary mechanism limiting the efficiency of non-viral vectors is the insufficient disruption of endosomal membranes to prevent nucleic acid degradation within lysosomes. To exploit this, one should design hybrid cationic lipid-polymer nanoparticles that incorporate specific endosomolytic components, such as pH-sensitive or cholesterol-modulating lipid domains. By optimizing the chemical structure to allow for rapid fusion or permeabilization of the endosomal membrane in response to the acidic environment, the system can release its cargo into the cytoplasm before it is destroyed, thereby approaching the intracellular delivery rates of viral vectors while retaining the safety and scalability advantages of non-viral platforms.
