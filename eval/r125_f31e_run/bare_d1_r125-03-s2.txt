**Mitigation Proposal: Dynamic Water-Cement Correction Loop Based on Aggregate Moisture Inference**

**Mechanism:**
Implement a closed-loop correction protocol that estimates the moisture content of the delivered aggregates in real-time by comparing the actual mass of the wet aggregate charge (from the batch scale) against the theoretical dry mass required for the specific batch design. The difference is attributed to free moisture. This inferred moisture value is then used to dynamically subtract an equivalent volume of water from the plant water addition setpoint for each batch. Simultaneously, use the mixer motor current profile as a secondary consistency validator to detect if the resulting slurry viscosity is outside the acceptable range, triggering an automatic hold-and-review status rather than proceeding to discharge.

**Implementation:**
1.  **Baseline Establishment:** Utilize the existing **plant air thermometer** and historical batch records to create a simple lookup table or linear regression model correlating ambient temperature, season, and known aggregate storage conditions to typical aggregate moisture content. This serves as the "expected dry mass" reference.
2.  **Real-Time Moisture Inference:** For every batch, the **batch scale** records the actual weight of the aggregate charge when added to the mixer. The system calculates the deviation between the *theoretical dry weight* (based on the mix design and the current estimated moisture factor) and the *actual scale reading*. The formula is: `Inferred Moisture Volume = (Actual Scale Weight - Expected Dry Weight) / Density of Water`.
3.  **Water Adjustment:** The plant control system automatically reduces the water dispenser setpoint by the `Inferred Moisture Volume` calculated in step 2. This compensates for the variable water content entering with the aggregates, ensuring the total water-to-cement ratio remains constant regardless of aggregate moisture fluctuations.
4.  **Current Profile Validation:** As the mixer operates, the **mixer motor current meter** monitors the load. A consistent concrete consistency yields a predictable, stable current profile. If the current profile becomes irregular (e.g., spikes indicating clumping due to too little water, or a sustained low load indicating overly wet slurry despite the correction), the system flags the batch. Instead of discharging, the batch is held for manual inspection or adjusted with a final small volume of water (if too dry) based on the operator's judgment guided by the current anomaly. This uses the existing current meter not for control, but as a safety gate to prevent releasing non-conforming concrete.

**Measures Relied On:**
*   **Batch Scale Records:** Provides the precise mass of each aggregate component, allowing the calculation of excess mass (moisture) relative to the dry mix design.
*   **Mixer Motor Current Meter:** Serves as a non-intrusive proxy for mixture rheology/consistency. An irregular or off-nominal current signature indicates that the inferred moisture correction was insufficient or excessive, triggering a quality hold.
*   **Plant Air Thermometer:** Used to contextualize aggregate moisture trends (e.g., higher temperatures may correlate with faster drying of stockpiles, lowering moisture), allowing the baseline "expected dry weight" model to be adjusted seasonally or daily, improving the accuracy of the moisture inference from the scale data alone.
