**Mitigation Proposal: Condenser Water-Flow Rate Verification and Optimization via Approach Temperature Monitoring**

**Mechanism:**
The observed wide condenser approach and high compressor kW/ton indicate that the condenser heat transfer is insufficient for the current heat rejection load. This is typically caused by reduced condenser water flow rate or fouling that cannot be detected without flow sensors. By utilizing existing condenser water supply and return temperature sensors, we can calculate the condenser's approach temperature (Condenser Water Outlet Temp – Condenser Water Inlet Temp) and monitor it in real-time. A rising approach temperature under stable load conditions correlates with reduced water flow velocity and degraded heat transfer. The mitigation involves verifying and, if possible, adjusting the condenser water flow rate to its optimal design value to minimize the approach temperature and restore COP.

However, the most direct existing measurement is the condenser water temperature differential $\Delta T_{cw}$. A low $\Delta T_{cw}$ at a given cooling load implies high flow (good) or low heat rejection (bad). A high $\Delta T_{cw}$ at a given load implies low flow (bad).
